La Margherita

Taken apart, slice by slice.

Five things go into it, and nothing else. Scroll and we'll show you every one.

01 · L'impasto

Dough that waits two days.

Caputo 00 flour, water, sea salt, a pinch of yeast. Then patience. It's opened by hand from the centre out. We never use a rolling pin.

48hcold rise before it's opened

02 · Il pomodoro

San Marzano, crushed by hand.

Grown in the volcanic soil under Vesuvius. Crushed between our fingers with a pinch of salt. No cooking, no garlic, no sugar.

03 · Fior di latte

Torn, never sliced.

Pulled by hand in Agerola every morning and on our counter by six, still warm from the whey.

04 · Il basilico

Five leaves, always five.

From the pots on our windowsill. They go on before the oven so they blister, and their oil runs into the sauce.

05 · Il fuoco

Ninety seconds in the oak.

485°C on the oven floor and one turn of the peel. The crust puffs and blisters into leopard spots, and the cheese only just melts.
