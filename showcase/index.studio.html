A small studio with long attention.

Norr & Sund is forty-two architects, engineers and model-makers across three cities. We take on a handful of projects a year and stay with each one from the first site walk to the last door handle.

We work mostly in exposed structure: board-formed concrete, glulam, brick that is left alone. Our buildings are meant to age well and to be cheap to keep.
