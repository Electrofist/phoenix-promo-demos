Chapter one

Summer carried us. A storm in November nearly didn't.

Monthly rides rose from 210K in January to a July peak, then fell off a cliff in November. Scroll to step through the year.

Rides follow the sun.

Every month from January to July beat the one before it. July hit 512K rides, about 16,500 a day.

Then, Storm Ilse.

Eleven days of flood closures along the harbour cut November to 196K, less than half of September.

Underneath, the fleet was changing.

Split by bike type, e-bikes grow every single month, from 31% of rides in January to 58% in December.

September: the e-bike majority.

For the first time, more people chose to ride with a motor than without one. They haven't gone back.
